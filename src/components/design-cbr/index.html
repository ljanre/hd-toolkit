Design CBR · Calculation Steps

  1. Compute Mean CBR

    x̄ = Σxi / nEq. (1)

    x̄ = Mean CBR (%); xi = Individual CBR (%); n = Number of completed tests; Σ = Sum.—

  2. Compute Sample Standard Deviation

    s = √[Σ(xi − x̄)² / (n − 1)]Eq. (2)

    s = Sample standard deviation (percentage points); xi − x̄ = Deviation from the mean.—Use n − 1 for the sample deviation, as shown in the reference.

  3. Determine Design CBR

    Design CBR = x̄ − ⅔sEq. (3a)

    x̄ = Mean CBR (%); s = Sample standard deviation.—The two methods are alternatives. Results are not combined, averaged or automatically clamped to zero.

  4. Estimate Resilient Modulus

    MR (psi) = 1500 × Design CBREq. (4a)

    MR = Roadbed resilient modulus (psi); Design CBR = Selected design value in percent, e.g. 6.30.—MR is roadbed resilient modulus, not subgrade reaction k. The 1500 correlation requires fine-grained soil with soaked design CBR ≤10, per your supplied reference.
