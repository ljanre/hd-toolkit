# Alignment reference review — 1 November 2026

The published DPWH plan notes for 25KI0043 distinguish a recommended 50 m tangent between reverse curves and a 30 m lower limit. They also discuss superelevation reversal, compound-radius compatibility, avoiding broken-back curves, consistency, small-angle kinks, and coordination with the vertical alignment.

Primary source: https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/dpwh/sites/default/files/webform/civil_works/advertisement/25ki0043_plan.pdf
Corroborating DPWH notes: https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/dpwh/sites/default/files/webform/civil_works/advertisement/25lj0107-_package_2.pdf

The official pages' indexed text was available; direct PDF retrieval was blocked by the DPWH gateway. The full DGCS Volume 4 was not retrieved. These are DPWH project-plan notes citing DGCS, not a claim of a complete manual review. Apply the governing project edition and approved exceptions.

Alignment scope: reverse gap <30 m fails; 30≤gap<50 m warns; ≥50 m passes this length recommendation alone. Superelevation transitions may require more length. Compound ratio ≤1.5 remains the numerical screen. A same-direction positive gap cannot be accepted solely because it exceeds 30 m: the 30 m broken-back flag remains a tool heuristic and every separated same-direction arrangement receives additional review. Additional checks explicitly require speed/superelevation/friction, sightline/obstruction, design vehicle and profile evidence rather than reporting unsupported passes.

The separate HSO tool and the legacy general engine scope retain their existing project screens. Only Alignment Checker applies the new 50 m recommendation tier.
