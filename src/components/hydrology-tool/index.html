    The Rational Method estimates a peak, not a flood hydrograph or storage volume. It assumes spatially uniform rainfall, full catchment contribution at Tc and matching rainfall/peak-flow frequency. Significant detention, floodplain routing, large basins and complex networks require an appropriate hydrologic method. Carry Q into the hydraulic design and check capacity, headwater/tailwater, freeboard or allowable spread, inlet capture, outlet erosion and the governing higher-flood condition. Rainfall uplift is not added automatically; enter the adopted project intensity once.
