DGCS prescribed curve widening · separate check

DGCS §3.6.2.4, pp.3-33–3-35: simple curves widen the inside edge; spiral curves may widen inside or split both edges. Tables 3-22–3-23 supply the WB-19 baseline and vehicle adjustments. Both scale by 1.5 for three lanes and 2 for four lanes. Values below 0.6 m may be disregarded. Only exact tabulated combinations are returned; blanks and unlisted vehicles remain TO BE ESTABLISHED. Modified/Custom dimensions have no automatic table adjustment. The reference width is the table's two-lane width, not total width of a three/four-lane roadway.

Method and design review

Rigid rear-axle radius = √(front-axle radius² − wheelbase²). For the articulated vehicle, trailer axle radius follows the kingpin radius and trailer wheelbase using the same no-slip circular geometry. Body corners include overhangs; calculated inner/outer limits are radial extrema of the rectangles. Additional inside/outside clearance compares that envelope with the lane centred on the front-axle path.

Geometric encroachment is not the prescribed design widening. Do not automatically transfer either result to the cross-section. Verify the approved vehicle, steering capability, speed, clearances, widening distribution and transition length. Use validated swept-path software for junction entry/exit manoeuvres and final approval.
