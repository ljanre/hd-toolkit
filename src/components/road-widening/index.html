Workbook calculation and input guide

u is the workbook track-width parameter; it is not necessarily full body width. ΣLi² is the workbook's signed segment-square sum, preserving negative and repeated segment factors. A is front overhang and L is the front-to-rear wheel parameter. Vehicle silhouettes are indicative and not dimensioned body envelopes.

Widening = MAX(ROUND(N × [u + R − √(R² − ΣLi²) + C] + (N−1) × [√(R² + A(2L+A)) − R] + 0.1V/√R − (N/2)Wn, 1), 0).

Minimum radius = MROUND(V² / [127(emax + f)], 5). Cells below minimum radius are blank, as in the workbook. Zero results may be displayed blank; invalid square-root domains show TBE. A positive C override takes precedence; blank or zero uses the exact workbook width/C table. The 5.4 m clearance is identified as assumed in the workbook. No interpolation of C or friction is introduced.

The Metric sheet stores emax = 8%; changing it changes the blank-cell cutoff. Width Wn remains the workbook's normal two-lane reference even for more lanes. Values below 0.6 m may be disregarded, but the computed values remain shown. This table is workbook-method output, distinct from DGCS tabulated widening and from the separate Swept Path Analysis tool. Confirm workbook provenance and approved project criteria before adoption.
