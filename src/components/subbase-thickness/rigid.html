Paragraph 47's base values assume no construction traffic uses the subbase. Damage risk on weak or normal subgrades adds 150 mm at CBR ≤4%, or 80 mm on other normal subgrades. Its upper-layer material provisions and any required surface regulation remain separate checks.

Paragraph 48 requires 450 mm total non-frost-susceptible construction where the soil is frost susceptible. This tool assumes the entered slab and proposed subbase are non-frost-susceptible. Any relaxation for low traffic requires separate local evidence and is not automated.

Table 5 gives minimums rather than a rigid subbase nomograph. Figure 6 remains available in the Asphalt / Flexible mode.
