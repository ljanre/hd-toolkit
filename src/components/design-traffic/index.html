Equivalence Factor · EF

Convert each vehicle loading case into equivalent standard axle loads.

Vehicle cases are linked to Section 1. Catalogue GVW and axle shares are read-only. Select New Configuration in Section 1 to edit GVW (kg) and fractions; shares must total 1 (rounding tolerance 0.001). Catalogue values are reference loading cases. Red arrows show axle loads. Labels give the fraction of W (gross vehicle weight) and the corresponding load in tonnes. EF is calculated automatically from GVW and axle fractions. Unverified or missing EF keeps W18 pending.

Distribution Factors

Adopt the design-direction and design-lane shares.

Share in the design direction (0–1, greater than zero). Use 1 for already directional counts.

Share in the design lane (0–1, greater than zero). Use 1 for already design-lane counts.

DD = 0.50 represents an equal two-way split. Select DL from the approved project criterion for lanes per direction. DD and DL are applied once to the sum of CESALᵢ. Counts already limited to the design direction/lane require factors of 1 for those distributions.
