Pavement and shoulder widths follow the schedule's project-adopted values, otherwise its baseline. The example's 3.05 m lanes and 2.5–3.0 m shoulders do not override selected criteria. Crossfall 2% and shoulder fall 3% are initial illustration values; select Normal crown or Superelevated curve. Superelevation rotates about the finished centreline; shoulders continue the same plane. The displayed rate is checked against the schedule’s maximum, but curve radius, side friction, shoulder rollover and transition/runoff lengths require separate design.

Ground levels are measured at the outer shoulder edges, relative to finished centreline. Initial +1 / −1 m values demonstrate mixed cut/fill only; replace them with survey values. Ground between and beyond those points is modeled as a straight line. Areas exclude pavement and use its underside as formation; add designed base/subbase separately. ROW, berms and drainage geometry need project dimensions.

Tourism Table 3-2: PCC 230 mm / 1.5% cross slope; ACP 50 mm / 2%; shoulder ≥1.5 m with gravel surfacing. These provisions are tourism-specific. Table 7-1 assumes berms and slope protection; granular fill assumes adequate foundation capacity and drained, unsaturated conditions. Rock cut slopes require §7.3 assessment. Material ranges are reference guidance, not stability approval. At exact band boundaries both ranges are shown for review. Do not use this simplified section for earthwork quantities or slope design without surveyed sections and geotechnical review.

Scope & Review Notes

Adopted values are retained when controls change. Reset clears them. For 95 km/h, Table 3-8 has no exact row: no stopping-distance interpolation is assumed. Table 3-1 non-passing distance and Table 3-8 stopping distance remain separate. Radius, grade and older passing-distance baselines are not recalculated for a changed speed.

Expressways need a divided, multilane section; the two-lane illustration is disabled. §3.1 requires at least two 3.6 m lanes in each direction; §3.4 specifies ≥80 km/h, even though Table 3-1 includes lower speeds. No contradictory provision is silently discarded. Tourism surfacing thickness and cross slopes are not applied to other road classes. Functional urban or unlisted cases require project review. National-road grade compensation and approval requirements are in §3.6.3.1 (p. 3-39).
